**A-race:** Mt. Fuji International Marathon · Sun 13 Dec 2026 · 09:00 · 6-hour cutoff with checkpoints

**Goal:** finish Fuji inside the cutoff. Tier 1 pacing 7:30/km to Saiko then 7:30–8:00 (≈5:40). Tier 2 fallback 7:50 flat (≈5:50–5:55).

## 1. Why the plan was rebuilt

Strava analysis of 268 activities (2 Oct 2025 → 6 Sep 2026):

- 1,226 km / 222 runs in 11 months; 3.6 run-days per week; only 1 zero-run week in 49.
- Real pace on GPS long runs improved 10:08 → 8:10 /km (Feb → Aug). Treadmill sessions overstate distance ~18–20 %, so indoor paces are not trusted.
- Long-run stopped time rose from 5 % (Dec–Feb) to 30 % (Jul–Aug). Elapsed pace on the 8 Aug half was 12:36/km — slower than the 7 Feb half despite faster running.
- Nine weeks exceeded 1.5× the trailing 4-week average. Both injuries this year followed such spikes: back + plantar fasciitis (Apr) and the current right lateral-foot pain after the 27 km run on 29 Aug (2.7× the longest run of the previous two weeks; cadence fell 157 → 130 spm; HR 162 avg = Zone 3 throughout; 56 min of stops).
- The 27 km run was in a shoe not worn for 35 days; 50 % of August mileage was in carbon/plated racing shoes.

The previous app plan (23 weeks, sub-5:00, POL→PYR with VO₂max intervals) assumed 45+ km/week in September and did not know about the injury.

## 3. Evidence base

From `~/brain-endurance` (pages cited by slug) plus literature outside the vault:

- **Return to running** — Daniels: never resume pre-break load; 33 → 50 → 75 % ramp for longer breaks; leg-involved cross-training (cycling) shrinks the fitness loss (`training-breaks-and-return-to-running-daniels`).
- **Marathon structure** — Daniels: two quality days maximum separated by 2–3 easy days; M-pace in long runs for specificity and fueling practice; treat a race as a Q day and drop the other Q that week (`daniels-marathon-training`, `daniels-training-intensity-system`). Fitzgerald: base-heavy, late-race slowdown is the problem to solve; Level 1 first-marathon plans peak ~6.5 h/week (`80-20-marathon-training-plans`).
- **Long-run length** — Higdon Novice 1 peaks at 32 km three weeks out; Galloway run-walk finishers go longer at low weekly volume; runners whose longest run is <30 km in the final 8 weeks hit the wall more often. Time-capped models (Daniels ≤2.5 h) assume higher weekly mileage than this athlete has. Decision: peak long run 28–30 km capped at 4:00, progression ≤1.25× per step.
- **Intensity distribution** — for recreational runners PYR ≈ POL; consistency matters more than the model (`training-intensity-distribution`, Rosenblat 2024). Hard/easy separation; avoid the moderate-intensity trap (`hard-easy-principle`, `moderate-intensity-trap`).
- **Cross-training** — cycling raises total aerobic volume without impact; the 80/20 split applies to run + bike time; hard work stays in running (`cross-training-for-runners`).
- **Bone loading** — strain magnitude, rate, and frequency drive adaptation; repetitive load plus low energy availability or unfamiliar equipment produces stress reactions; adaptation reverses when loading stops (`bone-adaptation-to-loading`).
- **Strength** — high-load (≥80 % 1RM) plus plyometrics is the only combination with a reliable running-economy effect; the effect is speed-dependent and small at marathon pace, but strength protects against injury and preserves late-race mechanics; 2×/week, ≥8 weeks (`strength-training-running-economy-speed-dependent`, `resistance-training-prescription-strength-hypertrophy`). Plyometrics: ES −0.31 at ≤12 km/h; tendon adaptation lags muscle, so volume rises slowly.
- **Taper** — amateur endurance: 20–30 % volume cut, keep race-specific intensity, don't over-taper (`taper-strategy`).
- **Environment** — Bangkok 28–32 °C training to a 2–8 °C race: marathon performance degrades ~10 % between WBGT 10 °C and 25 °C for 3 h+ runners (Ely 2007), so the same effort should be 6–10 % faster at Fuji. Cold suppresses thirst; dress in removable layers; no heat-acclimation work is needed (`environment-and-altitude-specific-training`).
- **Load monitoring** — ACWR 0.8–1.3 sweet spot, >1.5 injury risk (Gabbett 2016); long run ≤1.25× the longest of the prior two weeks; long run ≤ ~50 % of weekly running for low-mileage plans.

Constraints verified in code (`tests/plan-engine.test.js`): weekly increase ≤15 % between non-down weeks; long run ≤1.25× the longest of the prior two weeks from W5; ≤2 quality sessions per week; W12 Saturday is the half; W14 Sunday is the marathon.

Weekly template (W4–W11):

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strength A | Easy (longest easy day) | Quality | Strength B + easy 3–4 km | Rest | Long run (morning) | Bike Z1 |

Long-run rules: continuous; from W7 a planned 30-second walk every ~2.5 km rehearses the race protocol; targets stop time <8 %, cadence decay <5 %, HR drift <8 %. If any target is missed, the next long run repeats the same distance.

Gate handling: `gateDate` is the first running day. If it slips by N weeks, the return block shifts right and weeks are removed from the middle — one week: W6; two weeks: W6 and W8 with the peak scaled to 41/45 km (long runs 21/26 km); three weeks: W5, W6 and W8 with W7/W10/W11 overridden to 32/36/40 km — so the race date, the longest run at D-22 and the half test never move. All of this is data-driven in `js/plan-spec.js` and `js/plan-engine.js`.

## 6. Strength and plyometric program

Two sessions per week (Mon A, Thu B), ≥6 h from the day's run. Loads are % of estimated 1RM; plyometric volume is counted in ground contacts.

| Weeks | Phase | Loads | Plyometrics | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Rehab / foundation | 60–70 %, 3×10–12 | none | foot intrinsics, peroneals, tibialis posterior, soleus/gastroc isometric → isotonic, hip abductors, trunk |
| 4–6 | Strength | 75–85 %, 3–4×5–6 | W5+: pogo hops, low box 20 cm; ≤80 contacts | back squat, RDL, split squat, hip thrust, heavy calf raise |
| 7–9 | Strength + descent prep | 80–85 %, 4×4–6 | drop jump 20–30 cm, bounding; ≤120 contacts | add eccentric step-downs and Nordic curls for the 35 km descent |
| 10–12 | Maintain | 80 %, 2–3×5 | ≤80 contacts, last plyo W11 | keep intensity, cut sets; W12 Monday only, light |
| 13 | Taper | 75 %, 2×5, Monday (D-13) only | none | last lift ≥10 days out (Rønnestad 2014) |
| 14 | Race week | none | none | mobility only |

Exercise lists live in `js/strength-program.js` with YouTube search links preserved from the current app.

## 8. Decision rules (also implemented as app guards)

1. **4 Oct (end of W4):** no continuous 9 km yet → choose compressed plan or fall back to the half as A-race.
2. Any week where planned km > 1.5× the trailing 4-week average is capped; above 1.3× is flagged (Gabbett 2016 caution/danger lines).
3. Foot pain >3/10 during or the morning after a run → three bike days, resume at the previous week's load.
4. **15 Nov (end of W10):** 24 km long run at elapsed ≤8:15 with stops <8 % not achieved → Tier 2 pacing and the half becomes the longest run.
5. W12 half sets the tier (see §7).

## 9. App changes

Zero-build architecture is kept: plain files served by Vercel, no bundler. New logic lives in `js/*.js` as classic scripts with a CommonJS export guard so `node --test` can import them.

- `js/plan-spec.js` — the plan above as data (athlete, paces, phases, weeks, races, checkpoints, decision rules).
- `js/plan-engine.js` — `buildPlan(spec, {gateDate})` → weeks with seven session objects each; `checkProgression(weeks)` returns constraint violations.
- `js/strength-program.js` — phase-aware session lists; `sessionFor(week, day)`.
- `js/intel.js` — pure functions: `stopPct`, `cadenceDecay`, `hrDrift`, `analyzeStreams`, `acwrKm`, `longRunJump`, `weeklyGuard`, `raceProjection`.
- `api/strava/streams.js` — GET `?id=` returns down-sampled time/distance/heartrate/cadence/velocity/moving streams using the existing token refresh.
- `index.html` — consumes the modules; new dashboard "Load & form guard" card; race-week view rebuilt around the checkpoint table and two-tier pacing; strength view driven by the program; periodization/profile/side-races/weekly/speed/long-run views updated for the new phases and races; Strava import keeps elapsed time and enriches long runs (≥15 km) with stream-derived metrics.
- `sw.js` — cache bumped, `js/` files pre-cached.
- `tests/` — plan engine, strength program, intel math, stream normalisation, plus the existing data-audit test.
- Deferred to after Fuji: Supabase account sync and the activity inbox from the 2026-07-13 design.
